HRD concordance in TCGA-OV (exploratory). Reproducible R analysis of a 15-gene HR/BER expression signature, genomic HRD score and overall survival.
Data (not included): (1) clinical and RNA-seq data from the cBioPortal study Ovarian Serous Cystadenocarcinoma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas), downloaded September 1, 2026; (2) HRD scores: file TCGA.HRD_withSampleID.txt from the NCI GDC page The Immune Landscape of Cancer (https://gdc.cancer.gov/about-data/publications/panimmune; file ID 66dd07d7-6366-4774-83c3-5ad1e22b177e; Thorsson et al., Immunity 2018;48(4):812–830, doi:10.1016/j.immuni.2018.03.023), downloaded November 26, 2026. Columns ai1, lst1 and hrd-loh are the three genomic-scar components, and HRD is their sum.
Run: place the data files beside the script, then Sys.setenv(HRD_COL='HRD'); source('analysis.R'). Seed 2026; R 4.6.1, GSVA 2.6.3 (outputs/sessionInfo.txt).
Notes: use 06_correlation_all_with_both_scores.csv for the correlation (n = 290); 02_correlation.csv covers only patients with survival data (n = 289). 11 patients with blank survival status and 3 with status but no survival time are excluded. Stage and residual disease are unavailable; models adjust for age, with grade as a sensitivity analysis. Findings are exploratory.
